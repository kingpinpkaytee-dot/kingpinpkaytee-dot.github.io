Artist Biography

DiggerMan (legal name: Dico Nhlakanipho Dube) is a South African independent recording artist, songwriter and creative visionary from Johannesburg, Gauteng. Born and raised in the Riverlea community on the west side of the city, his music is rooted in the everyday stories, energy and resilience of his neighbourhood.

Operating under The Kingpin Entertainment Pty Ltd, DiggerMan presents himself as a storyteller whose work carries the banner MUSIC. PASSION. LEGACY. His visual identity and Riverlea West Archive frame his artistry as a living document of West Johannesburg culture — music, photography, cover art and neighbourhood spirit.

As an independent newcomer artist, DiggerMan is actively building his catalogue. Key early releases include the Genesis Flow series, the EP Riverlea Ext 9 (1.0), the single Sky, and the EP Fairy Tales (1.0). His music is live and searchable on Spotify.

DiggerMan: Turning Riverlea Stories Into a Living Sound

From the west side of Johannesburg, an independent artist is building a catalogue shaped by place, persistence and the belief that every real story deserves a soundtrack.

There is a particular kind of honesty in music made close to home. For DiggerMan, that honesty begins in Riverlea, West Johannesburg, and travels outward through a growing Spotify catalogue.

Born Dico Nhlakanipho Dube, DiggerMan approaches music as both expression and archive. His work reflects everyday life while making room for imagination, ambition and personal reflection.

His catalogue includes the Genesis Flow series, Riverlea Ext 9 (1.0), Fairy Tales (1.0), Sky, The Return Of Uncle Tom, Night Life, Uncle Tom, King Julius and additional releases.

For DiggerMan, independence means owning the narrative around the music: artwork, archive, visual language and the relationship with the community that inspired it. Through The Kingpin Entertainment Pty Ltd and the Riverlea West Archive, he is building a wider creative world around the songs.
