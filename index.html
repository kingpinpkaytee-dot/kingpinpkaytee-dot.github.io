Artist Biography

DiggerMan (legal name: Dico Nhlakanipho Dube) is a South African independent recording artist, songwriter and creative visionary from Johannesburg, Gauteng. Born and raised in the Riverlea community on the west side of the city, his music is deeply rooted in the everyday stories, energy and resilience of his neighbourhood.

Operating under The Kingpin Entertainment Pty Ltd, DiggerMan presents himself as a storyteller whose work carries the tagline MUSIC. PASSION. LEGACY. His visual identity and archive project (Riverlea West Archive) frame his artistry as a living document of West Johannesburg culture — music, photography, cover art and neighbourhood spirit.

As an independent newcomer artist, DiggerMan is actively building his catalogue. Key early releases include the Genesis Flow series, the EP Riverlea Ext 9 (1.0), the single Sky, and the latest EP Fairy Tales (1.0). His music is now live and searchable on Spotify.
