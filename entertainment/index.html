The Company

The Kingpin Entertainment Pty Ltd is an independent South African entertainment and creative company focused on developing artists, music releases, visual media, digital presence and original creative projects.

The company provides the professional framework around DiggerMan and future creative work. Its online presence is designed to provide an independently controlled, verifiable public reference for the company and its projects.
